Not Athletes Train — retail client
Bundle ID: com.notathletes.client (Android applicationId: same pattern)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Train Seriously. Your Way.

Not Athletes is a training app for structured, intentional training with a coach.

Subscribe to unlock structured programs, workout and nutrition logging, progress tracking, and direct coach communication. Connect with a coach in the app to get started.

All features require an active subscription. If your subscription ends, access is paused until you resubscribe.

The app removes distractions, gimmicks, and social pressure so you can focus on execution, consistency, and long-term progress.

Key Features (All Subscription-Gated)

Structured Programs – Programs for strength, conditioning, and performance.

Workout & Nutrition Logging – Log sessions, sets, reps, weights, and nutrition quickly and clearly.

Progress Tracking – Track performance over time and review past sessions.

Coach–Client Communication – Accountability and ongoing guidance from your coach.

Minimal, Focused Design – No social feeds. No gamification. Just training.

Not Athletes Coach — retail coach
Bundle ID: com.notathletes.coach
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The companion app for coaches who train clients on Not Athletes Train.

Assign programs, build and manage workout templates and plans, review client logs and progress, set nutrition templates, and stay in touch with clients through in-app messaging and calls. Built for clarity and focus—no social feeds, no gamification.

This app does not sell subscriptions. Your clients subscribe in the Not Athletes Train app; you use this app to deliver coaching.

Key Features

Client roster and assignments – See who you coach and open their training context quickly.

Programs & routines – Create and organize templates and plans for assigned clients.

Workout & nutrition visibility – Review what clients log and how they are progressing.

Coach–client communication – Messaging and calls with assigned clients (where enabled).
