**Train Seriously. Your Way.**

Not Athletes is a training app for structured, intentional training with a coach.

Subscribe to unlock structured programs, workout and nutrition logging, progress tracking, and direct coach communication. Connect with a coach in the app to get started.

All features require an active subscription. If your subscription ends, access is paused until you resubscribe.

The app removes distractions, gimmicks, and social pressure so you can focus on execution, consistency, and long-term progress.

**Key Features (All Subscription-Gated)**

- **Structured Programs** – Programs designed for strength, conditioning, and performance.
- **Workout & Nutrition Logging** – Log sessions, sets, reps, weights, and nutrition quickly and clearly.
- **Progress Tracking** – Track performance over time and review past sessions.
- **Coach–Client Communication** – Accountability and ongoing guidance from your coach.
- **Minimal, Focused Design** – No social feeds. No gamification. Just training.
